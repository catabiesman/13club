---
title: "Honorary Members"
---

Respectable members such as Judge McAdams served another purpose: they lent the Club publicity, legitimacy, and respectability. 

The Club expanded this arm of their publicity campaign by extending honorary membership invitations to an array of federal and state government officials and politicians. Eventually, the honorary membership would boast all of the US presidents who served from 1882-1909: Chester Arthur, Grover Cleveland, Benjamin Harrison, William Mckinley, and Theodore Roosevelt. While the Club enjoyed the notoriety and social currency of such illustrious honorary members, no evidence suggests that these Presidents ever attended a Club dinner. 

Nevertheless, it is significant that these Presidents and other political men of the time apparently judged honorary membership to the Thirteen Club as beneficial--or at the very least, not damaging--to their public reputations.

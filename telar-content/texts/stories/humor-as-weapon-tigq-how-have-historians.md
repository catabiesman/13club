title: "How have historians studied emotions? "
---

Ahmed's case studies demonstrate that emotions operate differently across social positions. For example, she explores how emotions like fear or disgust are not applied equally to all bodies, but instead 'stick' to certain bodies in ways that reinforce or even create power hierarchies. The Thirteen Club presents an opportunity to interrogate how and in what ways humor informs similar formations of identity and power. 

Want to explore other approaches to the study of emotions in history in greater detail? The [glossary](https://catabiesman.github.io/13club/glossary/) outlines several other approaches to the study of emotions in history.

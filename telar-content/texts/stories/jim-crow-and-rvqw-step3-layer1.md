---
title: "Unpacking the cartoon"
---

Like the previous cartoon featured in the same Thirteen Club report, this cartoonist illustrated a Black body in a child-like pose. You'll also notice that the dice--indicating games of chance--has landed on 13. 

That the cartoon is otherwise sparse on further indicators of any particular superstition beyond the presence of the number 13 beside a Black body demonstrates the stickiness with which Blackness and superstitous fears were correlated within Club discourse--and perhaps even broader society.

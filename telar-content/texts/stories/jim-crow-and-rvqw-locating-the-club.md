---
title: "locating the Club in time "
---

The Club was established in 1882--a mere twelve years after the final Reconstruction Amendment passed in 1870 which granted Black men the right to vote. The Club's founder, William Fowler, was himself a Civil War veteran of the Union Army.  

Why does this matter for our understanding of the Club's sense of humor? 

By repeatedly affiliating masculine intellectualism with humor and happiness, the Club effectively rendered humor itself as exclusive to White men. Their repeated rhetorical and visual efforts to suggest that superstitious people were unhappy cowards--"slaves" to superstition--further contributed to this discourse.
